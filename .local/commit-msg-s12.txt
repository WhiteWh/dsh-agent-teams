WP6.4: requiredReviewers is enforced (S12)

Planning section 6.4 (recommended option): enforce the field rather than delete it.

Rule: canDeclareDelivery requires, for every entry of reviewPolicy.requiredReviewers,
at least one review task that is completed with verdict=pass whose reviewer matches -
the member name equal to the entry case-insensitively, or the reviewer member's role
containing the entry as a substring, so "correctness" matches
"correctness-reviewer". That follows the spec sentence "known role aliases or member
names". A review the captain owns satisfies nobody: the captain is not an independent
role. The blocker is: no passing review from the required reviewer "<entry>"
(reviewPolicy.requiredReviewers). An absent or empty list keeps Delivery open, so no
existing team is retroactively blocked.

Tests first: two of the five new group-Q labels failed (the missing role produced no
blocker at all, and a captain-owned review counted as a role). Green now:
quality-gates-tdd 132 PASS/0 FAIL, verify.mjs 237 PASS/0 FAIL, all 21 suites exit 0.

Fixture change, recorded because it touches shared setup: the shared team() helper in
the tdd suite carried an inert requiredReviewers: ['correctness', 'security', 'scope']
while no fixture team staffs a security or scope reviewer. With the field live, every
unrelated Delivery check (dead tasks, waivers, supersession) would fail for a policy it
never modelled. The default list was removed - no assertion was touched - and group Q
sets the policy explicitly for each case it claims.

Build trap (second occurrence): the first build failed on reviewer.role being optional
(TS18048) after clean-build had deleted lib/, and the tdd suite reported green against
the partially emitted tree. Fixed the narrowing, rebuilt to exit 0, then trusted the
suite.

Docs: quality-gates.md section 5.3 documents the enforcement and the matching rule,
usage.md states it next to the profile example, README gained the capability row and
dropped the field from the remaining-plan list.
