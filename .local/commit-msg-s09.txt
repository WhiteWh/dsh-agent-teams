WP3: supersede a lane that will not finish (S09)

Feedback section 3: a red lane could only be removed by takeover plus cancel. The
failed row stayed in the graph, descendants waited forever on a task nobody would
complete, and a review kept pointing at the dead id.

Rule (src/state.ts, src/types.ts):
- New terminal status `superseded` and the field TeamTask.supersededBy.
  TASK_TRANSITIONS gains the transitions into it from every non-completed status;
  superseded itself is terminal.
- unsatisfiedDependencies resolves a supersession chain recursively (cycle-safe):
  a dependency on a replaced task is satisfied by its replacement's fate, so
  history that was never rewritten cannot fence a lane forever.
- applySupersession(team, oldId, newId) is the pure operation: marks the old task
  superseded, drops its capability and changedPaths (output stays as history),
  redirects every non-terminal dependent's dependencies, retargets every
  non-terminal reviewedTaskId/sourceTaskId, and refuses a replacement that
  transitively depends on the task it replaces (dependency cycle).
- Shared status sets in types.ts: DEAD (cancelled/superseded), OPEN, SETTLED, so
  the server and the panel stop spelling out their own status chains.

Delivery (src/quality-gates.ts): superseded is excluded from blockers exactly like
cancelled (non-quality loop, "all work was cancelled", quality loop), and
unconfirmedWaivers skips dead work - history without a permanent delivery block.

Tool (src/tools.ts): new captain-only agent_teams_supersede_task (task_id, reason,
replacement, plus the create_task fields for the inline path). One locked
operation: create-or-resolve the replacement, apply the redirect, write, revoke the
previous owner's activation, emit agent-teams/task-superseded, kick the scheduler.
TEAM_TOOL_NAMES grows to 15 and capabilities.test.mjs pins the new count.

UI: VisualTaskState and taskTone gain superseded, the panel draws it in its own
faded striped grey rather than the error colour with the task.status.superseded
label in both locales, the progress summary reports it separately in the final
line, and a client-side settledTask() replaces the literal status chains.
